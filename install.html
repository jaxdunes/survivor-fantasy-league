iPhone Setup Guide

Safari PWA Installation

iOS Guide
1

Open in Safari

Must be Safari!

Must use Safari browser on iPhone (Chrome/Firefox on iOS do not support Add to Home Screen).

2

Go to Website URL

jaxdunes.github.io/survivor-50-fantasy-league

Already here? Excellent! Proceed to step 3.

3

Tap Share ⬆️ → "Add to Home Screen"

Tap the Share button (⬆️) at the bottom of Safari, scroll down, and tap "Add to Home Screen".

💡 Tip: App name defaults to "Fantasy Survivor" — don't change anything, just click "Add"!
4

Open App from Home Screen

Locate the new Fantasy Survivor app icon on your iPhone home screen and tap to open it.

5

Notifications → Enable Notifications → Allow

Inside the app, navigate to Notifications, tap "Enable Notifications", and select "Allow" when prompted by iOS.
